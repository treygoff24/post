## H. Suspected product bugs

- S1 (low, untested): `admission_checks` calls `revive_locked` (bridge.rs:368) before `ensure_route_allowed` (:395) and before the ended check. A letter that is then rejected `blocked_route`, or a first delivery to an ended participant whose record sits in the archive, still moves the recipient out of the archive (a write on a refused letter). :1105 pins only the `forged_from` ordering. Impact is small (recipient restored, letter refused), but it breaks the doc's own principle that "nothing is written" on a rejection. Unverified by execution.
- S2 (nit): `channel_relay_status` (bridge_topology.rs:331-335) folds every `read_regular` failure (symlink, directory, oversize, I/O error) into `LocalOnly("no bridge config")`, whereas `load_config` (:46) distinguishes absent from untrustworthy. The message then tells the sender the send stayed local when the config is merely unreadable. Not covered by a test.
- S3 (nit): the doc comment at `bridge_topology.rs:182-186` describes `bridge_health` but is attached to `MAX_CLOCK_SKEW` (:187), leaving `bridge_health` (:189) undocumented.
