## Suspected product bugs

- S1 (low, inconsistency, untested): `continue_lineage` calls `lineage::validate_name` at `lineage_store.rs:353` before taking the participants lock (`create` was fixed to validate under the lock, `2f86ce0`). Beyond the lock ordering, it also refuses to `continue` an existing lineage whose name later collided with a registered room, while `load`, `leave`, `withdraw`, and `send --to lineage:` deliberately tolerate that (P1, L9, L16). Whether joining should be refused is a policy call; no test states either answer. Unverified by execution.
- S2 (nit): `withdraw_current_voice` (:572-613) journals `voice_withdraw` after the gap is settled; a crash between the two leaves a settled withdrawal with no journal line. Doc says the journal is never a decision input, so this is by design; noted only because L8/L20 assert one journal line for the recovery paths but nothing asserts one for a crash after settle.
