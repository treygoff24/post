## F. Suspected product bugs

None found in the fence logic itself. Observations:
- `migration_fence.rs:596-679` `classify_write` ends in `_ => false`: a new `Command` variant that writes is silently a reader until someone adds it, which fails open. Not a bug today (all current writers are enumerated, and `Bridge` takes its own admission at `bridge.rs:103`), and no test can catch a future omission short of a compile-time exhaustive match.
- `tests/cli.rs:7503-7518` (test defect, not product): the matrix's `search_unavailable` branch is dead and its `assert!(!stdout(&chat)...contains("READ THIS FRAMING FIRST"), "non-empty text chat must render its read framing")` has a message that contradicts its assertion (7499).
